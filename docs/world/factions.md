# The Factions & the Unbound

*From* `#crucial-player-info` *· posted by Bri*

Every character in Eres stands with one of three powers. For the full story, see [The History of Eres](index.md).

## <span style="color:#4d7fe0">●</span> The Order of Valor

Defenders of the realm, dedicated to preserving the sanctity of the land and protecting its people. They are bound by a code of **justice, honour and balance**, and seek ancient arcane secrets to prolong life and secure the prosperity of Eres, not for power.

**Cities:** see [Order of Valor territory](cities.md#the-order-of-valor).

## <span style="color:#e04848">●</span> The Legion of Corruption

A dark and formidable force driven by greed and a lust for power. The Legion rules through **fear, manipulation and dark magic**, and seeks the deepest arcane secrets to extend its own existence and bend Eres to its will.

**Cities:** see [Legion territory](cities.md#the-legion-of-corruption).

## <span style="color:#d9a520">●</span> The Unbound

The Unbound hold the **neutral cities** of Eres: safe zones where members of every faction can meet without fear of hostility. See [Neutral Cities](neutral-cities.md).

**Cities:** see [Unbound territory](cities.md#the-unbound).
